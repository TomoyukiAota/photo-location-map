# Left open after the review: Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Korean, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malay, Hindi

From the report of round 7, the last one, for these eight languages: what the reviewers could not
confirm, what they left because two wordings are equally good, and what is the owner's to decide. A speaker
of one of these languages can settle most of it at a glance. The decisions that were made, with their
sources, are in `decisions-asia.md`.

## Equally good, left

Besides those that rounds 3 to 6 listed, which stand. Each of these was counted in this round, so that a later round
need not do it again. A count is written "the file's wording : the other".

- **"Location data" in the two Chinese files** (`位置数据`, `位置資料`; five texts each). Apple and Chromium have
  only `位置信息` and `位置資訊` (Apple 0 : 12 and 0 : 11, Chromium 0 : 39 and 0 : 33; Apple's Photos: `包含位置信息`,
  `无可用的位置信息`), but their English says "location" or "location info" in every one of those texts. Where
  the English says "location data", Android has the files' words (see the table above), and in the files the term
  stands beside `EXIF 数据` and is explained as `（纬度和经度）`. So the files translate the English word, and the
  other is the word of "location information". Not all on one side once the English is looked at. Left; a speaker
  may prefer `位置信息` / `位置資訊`, the more everyday of the two, which Korean, Hindi and the Japanese have.
- **Loading in Korean** (`불러오는 중...`, `불러왔습니다`, `불러올`, `불러온`: twelve texts of the application and
  Photo Data Viewer). The systems and browsers have the loanword: `불러오` : `로드` is 3 : 630 in macOS, 0 : 374 in
  Chromium, 0 : 21 in Android's Settings and 0 : 86 in ten files of Firefox. But the file's is the Korean verb for
  loading a file, and it is in applications of the same kind: GIMP has it alone (12 : 0, `미리 보기 불러오는 중...`
  for "Loading preview..."), VLC has both (10 : 26, `Flowplayer 불러오는 중...`), and macOS has `이미지 불러오기`
  for "Load image". A speaker would not call `로드 중` an improvement. Left.
- **Total in Simplified Chinese** (`loadedFiles.total`: `合计`, in the status bar's `JPEG: 3 / HEIF: 1 / 合计: 4`).
  `合计` : `总计` is 0 : 2 in macOS, 0 : 1 in Chromium, 0 : 2 in Android's Settings and 0 : 5 in VLC, each for the
  bare "Total". `合计` is the ordinary word for the sum of the items listed before it, which is what the status bar
  shows, and the Japanese has `合計`; Traditional Chinese has `總計`. Left; a speaker may prefer `总计`.
- **Advanced in Hindi** (`menu.help.advancedMenu`: `उन्नत मेन्यू`). Apple has `एडवांस` (107 texts), Google `ऐडवांस`
  (Chromium 4, Android 6), VLC `उन्नत` (13 texts, `उन्नत विकल्प दिखाएं` for "Show advanced options") beside
  `विस्तृत`, GIMP `विस्तृत`. Four words in use and none established; the file's is, from knowledge, Windows' too.
- **"latitude and longitude" in Korean**: `위도와 경도` on the website and `위도 및 경도` in Photo Data Viewer (two
  texts). The same two words joined by two conjunctions of the same meaning; as round 4 left Traditional Chinese
  `託管在` / `託管於`.
- **"fetches" in Vietnamese, Indonesian and Malay**: `đọc`, `membaca` ("reads") in the website's
  `landing.showsLocationsOnMap` and `lấy`, `mengambil` ("takes") in Photo Data Viewer's `about.keptInBrowser`, where
  the other five languages and the Japanese have one verb. Both say what happens, and neither sentence is the other
  one.
- **`settings.cache.usedNextTime` in Indonesian** (`Selanjutnya, cache digunakan …`): `Selanjutnya` is "from then
  on", and at the start of a sentence also "next"; after the sentence before it, it reads as the first. `Mulai lain
  kali` would be free of the other reading.

## For the owner

- Nothing needs a decision.
- The Thai, Malay and Hindi button names, Malay `Penjelajah Fail` and Hindi `फ़ाइल एक्सप्लोरर` can only be read off
  a Windows in the language. Seven rounds have now looked for them on the web.
- Choices that a speaker may want to make, each recorded above with its counts: Simplified and Traditional Chinese
  `位置数据` / `位置資料` or `位置信息` / `位置資訊` for "location data"; Simplified Chinese `合计` or `总计`; Korean
  `불러오는 중` or `로드 중`; and, from round 6, Malay `Guna` or `Gunakan` and Traditional Chinese `照片` or `相片`.
- Convergence in this group: 58, 46, 14, 3, 2, 2, 0 texts. The kinds of finding that earlier rounds lived on are
  used up: the words given "from knowledge" are all read from the products now, except those that need a Windows
  in the language; and of the pairs counted in this round, none had every source on one side once the English of
  those sources and the applications outside Apple and Google were looked at. I expect a further round to find
  nothing in these eight languages, unless a speaker or a Windows in Thai, Malay or Hindi gives it something new.
