# Left open after the review: Czech, Slovak, Polish, Croatian, Slovenian, Serbian (Latin), Lithuanian, Latvian

From the report of round 7, the last one, for these eight languages: what the reviewers could not
confirm, what they left because two wordings are equally good, and what is the owner's to decide. A speaker
of one of these languages can settle most of it at a glance. The decisions that were made, with their
sources, are in `decisions-central.md`.

## Equally good, left

- New in this round: **`lv` quotation marks `„…”`** against `“…”` (above).
- New in this round: `lv` `Faktiskais lielums` against Chrome's `Faktiskais izmērs`; `lt` `Tikrasis dydis`
  against Firefox's `Tikras dydis` and Chrome's `Faktinis dydis`.
- New in this round: `cs` `Windows omezuje` against `Systém Windows omezuje` (Microsoft's older, fuller way of
  naming it, and what Polish has here); both are Microsoft's.
- New in this round: Photo Data Viewer's `about.usageData` and `settings.usageData.send` in `cs` and `sk`
  say "the application" (`Kvůli vylepšování aplikace`, `aplikaci vylepšit`) where the English and the
  application's own `welcome.usageInfoCollection` say "this application" (`této aplikace`). The page has no
  other application to mean, and the sentence goes on with `o jejím používání`: nothing is lost.
- Read again and left as earlier rounds left them, with nothing new for or against: everything under "Equally
  good, left" of round 6's report (the case of Explorer's name in `lt` and `lv`; `lt`
  `Anuliuoti` / `Perdaryti`; `sk` `Vzdialiť`; the View and Selection menus where macOS and Windows differ;
  `menu.app.about` in `hr` and `pl`; `lt` `Slėpti kitas`; `sr-Latn` `Sakrij ostale`, `Podešavanja`,
  `Po imenu` beside `Naziv datoteke`; `sk` `Ďalšie možnosti`, `vybratý` / `vybraný`; `hr`
  `Prikaži pojedinosti`, `Zakreni`, `Poništi zumiranje`; `cs` `Určit oblast vrcholy`, `Další volby`;
  `pl` `Położenie pamięci podręcznej`, `miniaturka`, `Nr`; `sl` `po shranjevanju nastavitev`; Google Street
  View's name in `sl`, `sr-Latn`, `lt`; "hosted on GitHub" worded in two ways between the website and Photo
  Data Viewer; `hr`, `sr-Latn` `Jezik nije moguće učitati.`; `lv` `Noklikšķinot uz pogas …`; the names of the
  date taken and the date modified where ChromeOS differs; ` - ` in `directoryTree.openedFolder` and Photo
  Data Viewer's `pageTitle.*`; `lt`, `lv` `sortByShootingTimeInfo.details.modifiedTime` without a word for
  "as an alternative").

## For the owner

- **One question, about typography, not wording: the Latvian quotation marks.** The files have `„…”`, which
  the Latvian Language Agency recommends and Microsoft Word writes; Latvian software interfaces (Microsoft's
  style guide for software texts, Chrome, Firefox, GIMP, GNOME, Google's policies) have `“…”`. I left the
  files as they are. If the owner prefers what other software shows, it is one mechanical change of the
  opening mark `„` to `“` in 13 texts of `lv` (2 on the website, 9 in the application, 2 in Photo Data
  Viewer; 15 pairs), with no word touched; it would also undo the first pass's change of Photo Data Viewer's two texts.
- Nothing else needs a decision from this round. The questions of rounds 4 to 6 stand as they were: Google
  Street View in Latvian (`Ielas attēls` in Google's own interface), the two guides' different rules for
  Google's names, the Latvian SmartScreen names, and a capital for Explorer's name in Lithuanian and Latvian.
