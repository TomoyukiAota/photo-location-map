# Left open after the review: Bulgarian, Russian, Ukrainian, Greek, Turkish, Romanian, Hebrew, Arabic

From the report of round 7, the last one, for these eight languages: what the reviewers could not
confirm, what they left because two wordings are equally good, and what is the owner's to decide. A speaker
of one of these languages can settle most of it at a glance. The decisions that were made, with their
sources, are in `decisions-east.md`.

## Equally good, left

Read again with fresh eyes, and left for the reasons the earlier rounds give; nothing new was added to the list
except the first two, which a fresh reader stops at and which are not errors:

- **`el`: `Εμφάνιση στον χάρτη των φωτογραφιών με άγνωστη ημερομηνία`** and **`he`: `הצגה על המפה של תמונות שתאריך
  הצילום שלהן לא ידוע`** (`chart.showDateUnknownPhotos`): the place stands between the verbal noun and its object,
  so "the map of the photos" can be read for a moment. Unlike the Bulgarian that round 5 changed, the place has its
  own preposition (`στον`, `על`) and cannot be the object, and the other order would attach "on the map" to
  "unknown" (`לא ידוע על המפה`). Round 5 looked at both and left them; so does this round.
- **`ar`: `المحمَّلة`** in the application (`الملفات المحمَّلة`) and **`المحمّلة`** in Photo Data Viewer
  (`الصورة المحمّلة`): the same word with and without the vowel mark on the doubled letter. Both are right spellings;
  the mark is optional.
- **`ro`: `citește`** on the website and **`preia`** in Photo Data Viewer for "fetches"; **`bg`: `Избор на снимка`**
  (Photo Data Viewer's button that opens the file chooser) beside `Избиране на …` for selecting in the application;
  **`ar`: `اختيار صورة`** (choosing a file) beside `تحديد` (selecting what is on the screen): two things or two
  equal words in each case.
- Everything under "Equally good, left" in rounds 3 to 6 is as it was.

## Still not confirmed

- **SmartScreen's names read from a screen**: none of `bg`, `uk`, `el`, `ar` has a picture or a page that quotes a
  Windows in the language. After this round: `ru`, `he`, `tr` are settled by pages that quote the dialog; `ro` has
  the link from a picture and the button by Microsoft's verb on the same picture; `uk` three notes; `ar` three
  notes for both names (six with another link); `el` two witnesses; `bg` Microsoft's machine-translated page.
- **Windows' own names of a photo's "Date taken" and a file's "Date modified"** were again not read from a Windows.
